Motor Testing & Validation · CASE/03

Testing and validation on PMSM, BLDC and induction motors

Worked on test processes for PMSM, BLDC, induction, and submersible-pump motors. The goal was to validate motor behavior technically while preserving measurement discipline, and to make the result reportable.

CASE/MOTOR-TEST
CategoryMotor Testing
MotorsPMSM·BLDC·IM
BenchDynamometer

Test practice

The work covered test processes for PMSM, BLDC, induction, and submersible-pump motors. For each motor type, procedure tracking, measurement, and performance evaluation were front and center.

Bench preparation, safety and procedure checks, instrument operation, and data recording were carried out in a defined order. Measurement results were compared across different motor types and operating conditions, turning test outputs into technical conclusions for performance validation.
